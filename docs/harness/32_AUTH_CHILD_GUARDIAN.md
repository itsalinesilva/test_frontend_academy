# 32 — AUTH / CHILD / GUARDIAN

## Adult signup
Google/email → verification → profile → title → preferences → Academy onboarding.

## Child signup
Guardian identity → child account → consent/permissions → Kids onboarding → restricted profile.

## Guardian relationship
Guardian can manage permitted profile, visibility, safety settings and view academic progress according to applicable policy.

## Data minimization
Do not collect unnecessary child PII. Avoid public contact information. No direct messaging.

## Transition
A child reaching the product's applicable age threshold can transition to a traditional experience according to policy while retaining eligible educational history.
