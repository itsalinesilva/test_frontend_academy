# 14 — COMMERCIAL STRATEGY

## Product principle
The free product must be genuinely useful. Monetization should increase depth, capacity, professional utility, teaching capability and convenience rather than making basic learning frustrating.

## Future entitlement families
Student Basic; Musician Full Studio; Teacher Room; advanced analysis; storage/compute; premium content/licensed curricula; institution/school capabilities; professional collaboration.

No prices are defined here.

## Conversion logic
Value moments: first completed course, first skill improvement, first recorded practice, first polished project, first published portfolio artifact, first credential, first Room creation. Upsell should occur after demonstrated value and never interrupt a critical learning flow.

## Strategic moat
Integration is the moat: Knowledge Graph + Practice telemetry + Evidence + Profile + Community + Rooms + Studio. Individual tools may exist elsewhere; the connected longitudinal record is the differentiated layer.

## Commercial safety
Do not lock user-owned files behind payment. Do not delete progress after downgrade. Do not make public profile visibility contingent on payment unless explicitly designed. Entitlement changes must be reversible.
