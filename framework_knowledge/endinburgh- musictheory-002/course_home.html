    Final Assessment Now Open

    Hello everyone,

    Believe it or not, we are now into the 6th week of the course and the final assessment is now open.  To get to it, just click on 'FINAL ASSESSMENT' in the menu bar.  The assessment for this course takes the following form (NB, this is different to last year):

    1. End of Course Exam - Part 1 (to be completed by the 14th of September). 
    This is a number of multiple choice questions related to specific elements of a given piece of sheet music. You will be permitted 3 attempts at this part of the assessment and your best attempt (i.e. highest grade) will count towards your exam score. 

    2. End of Course Exam - Part 2 (to be completed by the 14th of September) 
    This is a number of questions that require discursive answers to questions about the same piece of sheet music. These answers will be peer reviewed and your mark for this part of the exam will be the median score awarded by your peers.

    3. Evaluate the work of three of your peers (to be completed by the 21st of September) 
    This is a mandatory component of the assessment and you will not receive a grade if you fail to complete this element of the exam.  You will be provided with detailed exam criteria in order to complete your reviews.

    Good luck everyone!

    The Fundamentals of Music Theory Team
